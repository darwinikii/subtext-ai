# Subtext AI

A personal reflective mirror that reads between the lines of your fragmented daily notes to reveal hidden cognitive patterns, unspoken avoidance, and blind spots.

## The Unique Kernel
While standard productivity apps focus on superficial, obvious cause-and-effect ("didn't drink coffee, got a headache"), **Subtext AI** detects subtle, hard-to-catch subconscious patterns, emotional undertones, cognitive conflicts, and personal **blind spots** that users fail to notice about themselves.

## Who It's For
Knowledge workers, developers, and creators who jot down scattered thoughts throughout the day, feel mentally drained or stuck, but cannot pinpoint what subtle dynamics are draining their energy or what tasks they are secretly evading.

## The Core Loop
1. The user drops 3-5 unfiltered, casual daily notes into an intimate messaging stream (or clicks a 1-click sample scenario).
2. Notes feed into a central dynamic "Mind Bubble" whose inflation reflects semantic insight density rather than raw count.
3. Once saturated, the bubble bursts (either automatically or on demand) to reveal 3 sharp, deep insight cards:
   - **Between the Lines (The Hidden Dynamic):** The unspoken common thread connecting seemingly unrelated notes.
   - **Your Blind Spot:** An unacknowledged defense mechanism, avoidance habit, or friction point.
   - **Deep Perspective & Micro-Shift:** A targeted cognitive exercise to break through that blind spot.

## Inspiration & Identity
- **Tone & Mood:** Candid, intimate, calming, and intellectually sharp—like an insightful personal mirror.
- **Visuals:** Sleek dark mode, glowing organic sphere, minimalist typography, and fluid particle transitions.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
The learner specifically wanted an app that doesn't just state the obvious, but "catches subtle, hard-to-detect details and deep connections." It also provides a hands-on experience of an AI coding agent adhering rigorously to a spec without drifting.

## What "Working" Looks Like
The user opens the app, enters 3 realistic fragmented notes (e.g., *"Hesitated and stayed quiet during the architecture review"*, *"Spent 2 hours reformatting documentation instead of writing the core module"*, *"Cancelled dinner with friends feeling completely drained"*). The bubble swells, reaches critical mass, bursts with a fluid animation, and produces 3 profound cards diagnosing a cycle of perfectionism and fear of criticism masquerading as fatigue. This 60-second sequence is the hero moment for the hackathon demo video.

## The POC Boundary
- **Now (In):**
  - Single focused screen with a messaging-style entry stream.
  - Interactive "Mind Bubble" that inflates according to semantic insight density.
  - Auto-burst trigger on critical mass + manual trigger with premature deflation warning.
  - LLM-powered deep insight engine extracting the 3 distinct cards.
  - 1-click "Load Sample Day" button for immediate demo verification.
- **Later:**
  - Multi-day historical journaling, memory persistence, and weekly pattern tracking.
  - Voice dictation for rapid thought capture.
- **Explicitly Cut:**
  - User authentication and login barriers (to ensure zero friction in demoing).
  - External calendar/task sync integrations.
