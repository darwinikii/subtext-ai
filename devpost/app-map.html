Subtext AI — App Map

Architectural Route & Takeaway Reference

Plain-Language Overview

Subtext AI is a reflective daily tool that reads between the lines of raw, fragmented thoughts. Instead of treating notes as a generic to-do list, it evaluates their semantic insight density (emotional markers, cognitive conflict, micro-avoidance) to inflate a central "Mind Bubble." At critical mass, the bubble bursts to unveil 3 diagnostic cards: Between the Lines (unspoken common denominator), Your Blind Spot (subconscious defense mechanism), and Deep Perspective (actionable reframe).

Architecture & Data Flow

The 3 Code Stops (Follow the Trace)

1
User Entry Dock & Event Handler — static/app.js (lines 140–180)

Listens for note submissions, sends text to /api/note, and recalculates the Mind Bubble's CSS transform scale and pulse frequency.

2
Linguistic Scoring & Analysis Engine — server.py (lines 35–85 & 120–190)

Evaluates emotional cues and cognitive conflict markers (0–40 pts). Connects to OpenRouter or engages the fallback engine to structure the 3 diagnostic dimensions.

3
Explosion Canvas & Diagnostic Cards Reveal — static/app.js & static/style.css

Executes the 2D particle burst animation, collapses the stage view, and reveals the glassmorphism diagnostic cards.

"To Change This, Start Here"

  • To adjust saturation speed: Change targetSaturation: 100 in static/app.js or modify scoring multipliers in server.py > calculate_semantic_weight.
  • To modify the demo scenario: Edit SAMPLE_NOTES in server.py.
  • To swap AI models: Update AI_MODEL in .env (e.g., meta-llama/llama-3.3-70b-instruct:free).

A Practice to Reuse

Plan-First Anchoring with Observable Verification: Instead of prompting the AI coding agent to "build a journaling app" (which leads to generic to-do clones and agent drift), we first specified the exact unique kernel, the semantic inflation formula, and observable acceptance criteria in scope.md and prd.md. Because every slice had a mechanical check, the agent stayed 100% on track and delivered a complete, working PoC in a single session.
